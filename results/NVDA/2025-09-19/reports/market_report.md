# NVIDIA (NVDA) Technical Analysis Report

## Executive Summary

NVIDIA's stock exhibits a complex technical picture with mixed signals across different timeframes. The stock is currently trading in a consolidation phase after experiencing significant volatility throughout the analyzed period.

## Detailed Technical Analysis

### Trend Analysis
**Long-term Trend (200 SMA: 140.70)**: NVDA is trading significantly above its 200-day moving average (176.24 vs 140.70), indicating a strong bullish long-term trend with approximately 25% premium. This substantial gap suggests the stock remains in a robust uptrend despite recent volatility.

**Medium-term Trend (50 SMA: 175.30)**: The current price sits just above the 50-day SMA, indicating neutral to slightly bullish medium-term momentum. The 50 SMA has been steadily rising from 165.66 to 175.30 over the past month, confirming the underlying uptrend remains intact.

**Short-term Momentum (10 EMA: 174.78)**: The 10-day EMA shows recent volatility, declining from peaks around 179+ in late August to current levels. The price (176.24) trading above the 10 EMA suggests short-term bullish momentum is attempting to reassert itself.

### Momentum Indicators
**MACD Analysis**: The MACD shows concerning momentum deterioration. After reaching strong positive levels above 3.7 in late August, it has declined dramatically to near-zero levels (0.018 on Sept 18). This represents a significant loss of bullish momentum, though the recent slight positive reading suggests potential stabilization.

**RSI Momentum**: The RSI at 53.37 indicates neutral momentum, having recovered from oversold conditions below 40 in early September. The RSI pattern shows:
- Oversold conditions in early September (36.73 on Sept 5)
- Gradual recovery to neutral territory
- No extreme overbought readings recently

### Volatility and Range Analysis
**Bollinger Bands**: The stock is trading within the middle portion of its Bollinger Bands (Upper: 183.97, Lower: 166.20). The current price position suggests neither breakout nor breakdown conditions, indicating consolidation.

**Average True Range (ATR: 4.97)**: Volatility has remained elevated around $5, indicating significant daily price swings. This level of volatility suggests active trading and potential for sharp moves in either direction.

### Key Technical Levels
**Support Levels**:
- Immediate support: 50 SMA at 175.30

**Resistance Levels**:

### Volume and Price Action
The stock has experienced elevated volume throughout the period, particularly during the August-September decline, indicating institutional participation in the move. The recent recovery on September 10-11 saw volume spikes above 220 million shares, suggesting strong buying interest at lower levels.

### Risk Assessment
The elevated ATR of 4.97 indicates traders should use wider stop-losses (approximately 2-3x ATR = $10-15) to avoid being stopped out by normal volatility.
